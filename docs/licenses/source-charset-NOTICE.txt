Source charset recognition, response decoding and MediaType parsing

The TypeScript default charset recognizers and sourceCharsetData.json are
derived from Legado's source-bundled ICU4J recognizers.
Copyright (C) 2005-2016, International Business Machines Corporation and others.
Copyright (C) 2016 and later, Unicode, Inc. and others.

The ISO-2022 state logic and frozen converter facts in sourceCharsetDecodeData.json
use ICU 77.1. The accompanying icu-Unicode.txt contains the Unicode license and
third-party notices distributed with that version.

Source-pinned OkHttp 5.4.0 MediaType regular expressions and parameter handling:
Copyright (C) 2013 Square, Inc.
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The complete Apache 2.0 text is
included in quick-transfer-Apache-2.0.txt in this same directory.

The JVM Charset comparison outputs are test evidence, not the production
conversion tables. Test-only Java/C++ tools are never linked into the app.

The GBK, GB18030 and Big5 encoder compatibility data in
sourceGbkEncodeOverrides.json, sourceGb18030EncodeData.json and
big5EncodeData.json are generated by comparing the ICU data above with
iconv-lite 0.7.3. sourceCharsetAvailability.json contains the encoding names
recognized by iconv-lite 0.7.3. txtLegacyDecodeData.json contains byte mapping
and GB18030 range facts generated from iconv-lite 0.7.3.
singleByteCharsetData.json contains single-byte mapping and alias facts
generated from iconv-lite 0.7.3. eastAsianEncodeData.json and the Japanese and
Korean entries in txtLegacyDecodeData.json are generated from the same version.
cp950EncodeData.json contains CP950 mapping differences from that version.
The UTF-7, CESU-8, and automatic UTF-16 compatibility behavior in
remainingCharsets.ts is based on iconv-lite 0.7.3.
