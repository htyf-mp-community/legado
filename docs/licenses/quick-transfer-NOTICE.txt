This distribution extracts tc/s2t.txt and tc/t2s.txt without content changes
into src/data/chineseConversionData.json. It ports the bounded longest-prefix
matching and character fallback of BasicDictionary/Trie into TypeScript.
Legado's own T2S exclusion list is applied with the source's maxLen semantics.
These adaptations were made for the HTYF migration on 2026-10-29.
